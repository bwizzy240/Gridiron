Model blends scoring differential (Pythagorean win expectation), season record, home/away-specific record, and recent form, then layers on rest since last game, travel distance/time-zone shift, and weather, with a calibration adjustment based on backtested results — it is not a guarantee and doesn't account for everything a sportsbook prices in. Schedule, scores and standings are pulled live from ESPN's public scoreboard where available; weather comes from Open-Meteo; moneyline odds come from Kalshi's public market data, with spread and total odds read from Kalshi's price ladder or, more reliably, a free Odds API key (see button above); starting-QB rating comes from ESPN's box-score leaders for each team's most recent game; the injury report loads live from ESPN when you expand a game's details panel. Any of these can silently fall back to a generated sample if the live source doesn't respond — nothing here is guaranteed. Use this as one input, not the whole decision.
